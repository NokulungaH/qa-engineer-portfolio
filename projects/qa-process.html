PROJECT 03 · QA PROCESS IMPLEMENTATION

CASE STUDY PUBLISHED

Build QA From Zero

An anonymized, fictionalized case study demonstrating how I would establish repeatable QA practices in a team where formal quality processes are limited.

Confidentiality

Inspired by professional experience establishing QA practices, but the product, people, data, risks, metrics and scenarios are fictionalized. No employer/client process, internal document or confidential information is reproduced.

30 / 60 / 90-day approach

Understand the delivery system and risks first; establish lightweight repeatable controls second; then automate, measure and continuously remove low-value process.

Operating model

Requirement testability → risk assessment → test design → API/integration and functional testing → exploration → regression → triage → release evidence → post-release learning.

Senior QA principle

QA does not own quality alone. The goal is to make risk and evidence visible early enough for engineering and product teams to make better decisions.
