PROJECT 03 · QA PROCESS IMPLEMENTATION

Build QA From Zero

QA process implementation

Scenario

A product company has developers, stakeholders and regular releases but no formal QA function.

Phase 1 - Discover

Assess the SDLC, requirements, environments, release process, current defects, production incidents, risks and stakeholder expectations.

Phase 2 - Establish

Introduce test planning, test case standards, defect workflow, Definition of Ready, Definition of Done, regression and smoke strategies.

Phase 3 - Risk

Prioritise testing according to business impact, likelihood and change risk rather than treating every feature equally.

Phase 4 - Improve

Introduce meaningful QA metrics, automation where appropriate, release criteria and continuous improvement.
