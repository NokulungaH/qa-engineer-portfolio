Exploration & Defect Analysis

Demonstrating investigation beyond scripted test cases: focused exploratory missions, evidence gathering and defects written around reproducibility and business impact.

Exploratory charters

Time-boxed missions cover assessment resilience, progress integrity and authorization boundaries. Each charter defines a mission and focus while preserving freedom to follow evidence.

Defect quality

Reports include preconditions, reproducible steps, expected vs actual behaviour, environment, evidence expectations and impact. Severity and priority are explicitly separated.

Important disclosure

The defect examples are fictional demonstrations of reporting quality. They are not claimed discoveries against an employer, client, SauceDemo or JSONPlaceholder.
